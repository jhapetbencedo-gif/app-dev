# 🌟 Hunter × Hunter

[Hunter × Hunter](https://wikipedia.org) is a critically acclaimed anime and manga series created by *Yoshihiro Togashi*. It follows a young boy named Gon Freecss as he strives to become a licensed "Hunter" and find his missing father.

---

## 🗺️ Why You Should Watch It
> "You should enjoy the little detours to the utmost. Because that's where you'll find the things more important than what you want."
> — *Ging Freecss*

---

## ⚡ Core Strengths
* **The Power System:** Known as *Nen*, it is widely considered the most logical and well-structured power system in all of anime.
* **Deconstruction of Tropes:** It takes classic shonen cliches and flips them on their head.
* **Complex Villains:** Antagonists have deeply human motivations, making them highly memorable.

---

## 🎬 Major Story Arcs
1. **Hunter Exam Arc:** Gon meets his lifelong friends and takes a grueling test to get his license.
2. **Heavens Arena Arc:** Gon and Killua learn the fundamentals of Nen.
3. **Yorknew City Arc:** A dark, mafia-themed arc centering on Kurapika's revenge against the Phantom Troupe.
4. **Greed Island Arc:** The duo gets sucked into a dangerous, Nen-driven video game.
5. **Chimera Ant Arc:** A massive, psychological masterpiece exploring the evolution of a monster species and humanity's dark side.
